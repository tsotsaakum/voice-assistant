# Tsotsa Kitchen hours and policy

Opening hours:

- Monday to Friday: 07:00–16:00
- Saturday: 08:00–13:00
- Sunday and South African public holidays: closed

Refunds: catering orders get a full refund if cancelled at least 48 hours before the event. Same-day canteen sales are not refundable.

Delivery: we only deliver inside eThekwini (Durban metro). We do not deliver to Johannesburg from this kitchen.

Bookings: a R200 deposit holds a catering platter. The deposit is part of the R650 platter price, not an extra fee.

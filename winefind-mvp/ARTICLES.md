## Label / menu recognition (scan stack)

1. **Single-Image Driven 3D Viewpoint Training Data Augmentation for Effective Wine Label Recognition**  
   Chen et al., 2024. ViT + triplet metric learning; one-shot wine label matching with 3D viewpoint augmentation.  
   - [arXiv PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2404.08820) · [Scholar search](https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=SINGLE-IMAGE+DRIVEN+3D+VIEWPOINT+WINE+LABEL+RECOGNITION)

2. **Making paper labels smart for augmented wine recognition**  
   Armandi et al., *The Visual Computer*, 2024. OCR-based AR wine recognition without a large label-image DB (~91% accuracy on Emilia–Romagna wines).  
   - [Springer](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00371-023-03119-y) · [Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=Making+paper+labels+smart+for+augmented+wine+recognition)

3. **Multi-Modal Large Language Model driven Augmented Reality Situated Visualization: the Case of Wine Recognition (SOLLAMA)**  
   Armandi et al., WACV Workshop 2025. Replaces OCR with Llama 3.2 multimodal for wine label AR.  
   - [CVF PDF](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/WACV2025W/VISIONDOCS/papers/Armandi_Multi-Modal_Large_Language_Model_driven_Augmented_Reality_Situated_Visualization_the_WACVW_2025_paper.pdf) · [Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=SOLLAMA+wine+recognition+Armandi)

## Taste / multimodal recommendation (palate + Find Wine)

4. **Learning to Taste: A Multimodal Wine Dataset (WineSensed)**  
   NeurIPS Datasets & Benchmarks 2023. ~897k Vivino label images, ~824k reviews, human flavor-similarity experiment; concept embeddings for flavor.  
   - [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.16900) · [PDF](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2023/file/170035f97007fdfa665880107b56f384-Paper-Datasets_and_Benchmarks.pdf) · [Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=Learning+to+Taste+WineSensed)

5. **soMLier: a South African wine recommender system**  
   *Journal of Wine Research*, 2023. SA wines + Vivino ratings; collaborative filtering + matrix factorisation + content-based explanations. Highly relevant for a Cape / Joburg beachhead.  
   - [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1080/09571264.2023.2184333) · [Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=soMLier+South+African+wine+recommender)

6. **A computational approach towards food-wine recommendations**  
   *Expert Systems with Applications*, 2023. Computational food–wine pairing recommenders.  
   - [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.eswa.2023.121766) · [Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=computational+approach+towards+food-wine+recommendations)

7. **Somm: A Wine Recommendation Engine**  
   Kumar, Kovalev, Nguyen, Arora — The Erdos Institute, 2023.  
   - [Scholar / citations](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=bQliVbYAAAAJ) · [Scholar search](https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=Somm+Wine+Recommendation+Engine+Erdos)

8. **Wine Recommendation System Using the Optimum Degree of Similarity**  
   Collaborative filtering on wine ratings (Advanced Science Letters, 2017).  
   - [Ingenta](https://www.ingentaconnect.com/content/10.1166/asl.2017.10460) · [Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=Wine+Recommendation+System+Using+the+Optimum+Degree+of+Similarity)

## Restaurant lists, markup & information asymmetry (smart savings)

9. **To share or not to share: An analysis of wine list disclosure by Swiss restaurant owners**  
   *Journal of Wine Economics*, 2023. Wine as experience/credence good; why restaurants hide lists; ~3× markup rule of thumb discussed.  
   - [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1017/jwe.2023.13) · [Cambridge](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-wine-economics/article/to-share-or-not-to-share-an-analysis-of-wine-list-disclosure-by-swiss-restaurant-owners/50EA68AD3A8CB8FBC9A7488BD50222B2) · [Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=To+share+or+not+to+share+wine+list+disclosure+Swiss)

10. **Restaurant Wines: Bottle Margins and the By-the-Glass Option**  
    Dearden, Guo, Meyerhoefer — *Journal of Wine Economics*, 2021. NYC restaurants; BTG associated with higher bottle margins.  
    - [PDF](https://wine-economics.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/01-Vol-16-Issue03-Restaurant-Wines-Bottle-Margins-and-the-By-the-Glass-Option-by-James-A.-Dearden-Xiaohui-Guo-and-Chad-D.-Meyerhoefer.pdf) · [Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=Restaurant+Wines+Bottle+Margins+By-the-Glass+Dearden)

11. **What are the determinants of wine price mark-up size?** (AAWE Working Paper 195)  
    Sommelier survey on restaurant mark-up drivers (fine dining positioning, hotel capture, experience).  
    - [PDF](https://wine-economics.org/wp-content/uploads/2016/04/AAWE_WP195.pdf) · [Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=determinants+of+wine+price+mark-up+size+sommeliers)

12. **Price Dispersion in Wines** (restaurant Bordeaux prices)  
    Competition can *increase* wine price dispersion when wine is an add-on revealed only after entry.  
    - [PDF](https://www.cresse.info/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/2021_ps7_pa4_Rockett.pdf) · [Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=Price+Dispersion+in+Wines+restaurant+Bordeaux)

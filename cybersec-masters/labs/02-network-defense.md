# Lab 02 — Network defense sense-making

**Objective:** Use your networking knowledge for **defense documentation**, not offense.

## Tasks (own lab network only)

1. Draw a trust-boundary diagram of your home/lab network (router, Wi-Fi, VPN, cloud).
2. On a VM you own, capture a short traffic sample of **your own** HTTPS browsing *or* use a provided public PCAP dataset for study (e.g. public research PCAPs) — goal is familiarity with Wireshark/tshark display filters for TLS handshakes and DNS.
3. Write three **detection hypotheses** (e.g. “sudden spike in DNS NXDOMAIN”) and what log you would need — do not implement attacks that generate them against live networks.

## Deliverable

Diagram + one-page monitoring plan (signals, retention, who responds).

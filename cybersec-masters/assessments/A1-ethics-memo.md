# Assessment A1 — Ethics & legality memo (20%)

**Length:** 800–1000 words  
**Standard:** Master’s professional writing

## Prompt

A friend asks you to “just quickly test” their employer’s VPN portal for weak passwords because “they’re cool with security.” Draft a memo to your friend explaining:

1. Why this is ethically and legally fraught without organizational authorization
2. What a proper authorization path looks like (RoE, change window, data handling)
3. Safer alternatives (official bug bounty, lab practice platforms, proposing an internal program)

## Rubric

| Criterion | Weight |
| --- | --- |
| Legal/ethical clarity (SA-aware + general principles) | 35% |
| Practical authorization advice | 25% |
| Alternatives & professionalism | 25% |
| Writing quality | 15% |

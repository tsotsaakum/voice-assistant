# Assessment A3 — Mini research plan (40%)

**Length:** 2–3 pages

## Required sections

1. Research question  
2. Related work (cite ≥5 items from `readings/SCHOLAR.md` or Scholar)  
3. Method (measurement / survey / design science / case study)  
4. Ethics & legality  
5. Expected contribution (defensive or governance — not a new exploit)  
6. 12-week timeline  

## Examples of acceptable questions

- How effective are secret-scanning pre-commit hooks in student repos?
- What NIST CSF subcategories are most neglected in SA SME self-assessments?
- Can menu-scan wine apps create new privacy risks (location + alcohol preferences), and what controls help?
